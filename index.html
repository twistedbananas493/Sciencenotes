Unit 4: Global Interactions & Technological Expansion (1450–1750)

Key Concept 4.1: Technological Innovations and Regional Integration. Transoceanic maritime reconnaissance led to cross-cultural exchange, economic shifts, and new trade networks across the Atlantic and Indian Oceans.

1. Maritime Technology Development

  • Astrolabe refinement and magnetic compass adoption from Asian trade routes.
  • Development of caravel, carrack, and fluyt vessels designed for ocean navigation.
  • Understanding of global wind and current patterns (e.g., volta do mar).

2. Economic Systems & Trade Companies

Mercantilist policies driven by European rulers led to state-sponsored joint-stock companies, such as the Dutch East India Company (VOC) and British East India Company.

Study Reminder: Be prepared to analyze primary source documents regarding silver flow from Potosí to Qing Dynasty China for Friday's quiz.
