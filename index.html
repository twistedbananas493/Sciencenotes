Unit 4: Earth Systems and Resources

Key Concept 4.1: Plate Tectonics and Soil Formation. Geological processes continuously shape surface topography and nutrient cycling across global terrestrial ecosystems.

1. Tectonic Plate Boundaries

  • Divergent boundaries lead to seafloor spreading and rift valleys.
  • Convergent boundaries form subduction zones and mountain ranges.
  • Transform boundaries create fault lines susceptible to seismic energy releases.

2. Soil Horizon Stratification

Organic material decomposes in the O-horizon, washing minerals downward into the B-horizon subsoil through leaching dynamics.
